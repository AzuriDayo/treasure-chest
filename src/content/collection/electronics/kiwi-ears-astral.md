## Product feel

The box is quite generic looking for the price of $367.19 pre-tax. Even the inner box I find quite dull. Despite saying that, it came with the best fitting eartips for my ears, and a decent amount of accessories, such as the carrying case and replacement speaker grills.

## Usage

These IEMs are currently my main ones I use directly plugged on my PC. I'm using it with a FiiO KA15 DAC on the 4.4mm balanced jack. I sometimes bring these out for my [Fiio Disc](/treasure-chest/collection/electronics/fiio-disc/) if I want a bump up in sound quality. For gaming on cs2, these help with noticing enemy locations very precisely. As for music listening, the sound quality is unparalleled to anything I ever owned. It really picks apart song sounds very well, separates the details between vocal and each instruments and gives the listening experience a new feeling for me. This can only be felt when listening to FLACs, as music streaming services tune down the audio quality.

## Overall

Knowing that the main budget focus was the IEMs themselves, I noticed the accessories' quality feeling subpar. This is a positive point for me, as I do not put value on side-accessories at all. My main focus is very much the experience of the product and it has delivered an amazing result. I am more than happy to be owning these earphones.
